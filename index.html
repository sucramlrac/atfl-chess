The NFA Diagram of a Chess Queen

How to use

Hover over the chessboard to see the interactive NFA diagram of a Chess Queen.

Explanation

The nondeterministic finite automaton Chess Queen, having only two characters in the alphabet which is 'move' and 'capture' whereas the possible states or 'positions' for both characters, or 'actions', is the same. Ofcourse, unless the transition is occupied by another piece, as stated by the rules of chess, the Queen always has the input move in this example.

Some insights that we can gather from this tool is that (1) at any nonfinal state, there is 1 to 3 transitions to the final state, and (2) from d5 and e5's 'cone' there are three final states the Chess Queen can transition to.
